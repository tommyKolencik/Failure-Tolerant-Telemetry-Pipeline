Verification and experiments
============================

Run from the project root. Helpers own isolated processes/data and preserve
artifacts. Unit tests use temporary databases and no physical serial device.

  python3 -m pytest tests/ -q
  python3 scripts/verify-agent.py --with-metrics --outage-seconds 30
  python3 scripts/verify-serial.py
  python3 -m benchmarks.crash_checks

Current-source verification (2026-10-15):
- 294 tests passed on macOS arm64 / Python 3.12.10.
- Real localhost HTTP: all 2,897 pending events recovered after a 30-second
  collector outage and agent SIGKILL. Exact accepted keys and canonical content
  reconciled across 3,920 final events; zero pending/quarantined events.
- Collector-readiness-to-recovery: 0.390 s, including drain-agent startup,
  one observation. Not a sustained benchmark or a general latency guarantee.
- Commands, environment and source fingerprints:
  verification-2026-10-15.md and agent-verification-2026-10-15.json.

Measured evidence (historical versions; exact source hashes/copies preserved):
- Dell uploader: 1,050 exact events, including 100 duplicate retries after a
  lost ACK and 50 recovered during an outage. uploader-verification.json.
- Mac CLI: 1,066 exact events across delivery, SIGKILL/restart, competing-agent
  refusal, and SIGTERM drain. agent-verification-mac.json.
- Mac host/demo: 3,944 exact events; 2,921 pending survived a 30-second outage
  and agent kill. Recovery 0.381 s includes drain-agent startup, one observation.
  metrics-verification-mac.json.
- Mac serial: 1,063 unique events, 10 late replays, reconnect/new boot IDs,
  50 pending recovered after kill/outage, and SIGTERM drain.
  serial-verification-mac.json. Input used pseudo-terminals, not physical USB.
- Collector COMMIT crashes: four cases, batches 1/100, 242 exact final rows
  (222 newly accepted + 20 setup). commit-crash-report.md.
- Synthetic 100/sec target: nine 60-second trials, 53,720 exact accepted events.
  benchmark-report.md.
- Synthetic 200/500 targets: eighteen 60-second trials, 303,142 exact events.
  Target 500 was not achieved; cap 1 grew a queue before draining.
  benchmark-rate-sweep-report.md.
- Demo overhead: three matched pairs, 36,000 requests, 18,000 enabled events,
  zero disabled telemetry. Median paired normal p95 +1.02 ms; CPU +2.27 ms/request.
  demo-overhead-report.md.

Performance commands:
  python3 -m benchmarks.run --dry-run
  python3 -m benchmarks.run --duration 2 --repeats 1 --rates 100 200 --plots
  python3 -m benchmarks.run --duration 60 --repeats 3 --rates 100 --plots
  python3 -m benchmarks.run --duration 60 --repeats 3 --rates 200 500 --batch-sizes 1 20 100 --plots
  python3 -m benchmarks.run --duration 45 --repeats 1 --rates 100 --batch-sizes 100 --outage-at 5 --outage-seconds 30
  python3 -m benchmarks.demo_overhead --requests 6000 --concurrency 4 --repeats 3 --slow-delay .1 --plots

--output must name a new directory. Default outputs live in ignored
benchmarks/results/. Curated JSON/figures in docs/ are shareable with the repo;
raw databases, ledgers, logs, samples, and source copies require separate retention.

A successful run reconciles complete accepted keys/content, local durable ACKs,
and zero rejected/missing/unexpected/conflicting/pending/quarantined outcomes.
Counts alone cannot prove completeness. Keep workloads within collector and
quarantine retention; pruning removes content and identity.

Timing/resource definitions: benchmark-walkthrough.md.
Crash/overhead implementation: failure-overhead-walkthrough.md.
Same-host process crashes do not establish power-cut/storage fault tolerance.
Synthetic Mac runs do not establish Dell/hardware capacity. Remaining work:
sync/retest on the Dell, confirm firmware contracts/baud, test physical USB and
sensors, and profile the synthetic target-rate shortfall if higher rates matter.
